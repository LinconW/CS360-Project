CS 360 Project Three - Stockroom

Suggested emulator test sequence:
1. Launch the app and create a new username/password.
2. Log out and log back in with the same credentials to verify persistence.
3. Add two or more inventory items.
4. Edit an item, increase/decrease its quantity, close/reopen the app, and verify values persist.
5. Delete an item and verify it remains deleted after reopening the app.
6. Open SMS alert settings. Test DENY first and verify inventory still works.
7. Re-open settings and grant SEND_SMS permission. Enter a test phone number.
8. Create or reduce an item to quantity 0 to exercise the SMS alert path.

Note: Some Android Emulator images do not provide a carrier/SIM capable of delivering outbound SMS. The app uses Android SmsManager and handles send failures without stopping inventory functionality.
